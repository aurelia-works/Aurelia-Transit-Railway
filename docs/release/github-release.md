**Aurelia Transit Railway 1.2.0** (`4.0.5+atr.1.2.0`): a maintained fork of Minecraft Transit Railway 4.0.5 for **Minecraft 1.20.1, Fabric**.

- Drop-in for MTR: remove the MTR jar and put this jar in its place. The mod id is `mtr`, so MTR 4.0.5 worlds and train packs keep working. MAGIC 1.5.1, MTR Rail Works 0.2.3 and MTR France Addon 2.0.1 were checked against ATR's code, but not yet launched with this build.
- **English only for now.** The text and the announcer voices are English.
- 1.2.0: plan stations and airports in Station Studio and build them with the wrench, with real city fronts and halls. Also new: road building with bus lanes, rural roads and highways; passengers who change trains; express trains first at junctions; clock-face timetables; three downloadable English announcer voices; a seven-group Aurelia panel; the map with homes and landmarks; and the Resource Pack Creator.
- Removed in 1.2.0: the in-game station editor screen, the minimap, road cars, crew roles, freight loading, ATR's driver key box and the Station Codex. Their items stay registered, so old worlds load.
- Operator signs use generic marks, not real logos.

Back up your world first. See CHANGELOG.md. Based on Minecraft Transit Railway by jonafanho (MIT).
