# Aurelia Transit Railway (Modrinth and CurseForge page text)

**Summary (one line):** A maintained Minecraft Transit Railway for 1.20.1 that plans and builds whole stations, airports and roads. Drop-in for MTR 4.0.5.

## Body
Aurelia Transit Railway (ATR) is a maintained fork of [Minecraft Transit Railway](https://modrinth.com/mod/minecraft-transit-railway) 4.0.5 for **Minecraft 1.20.1 on Fabric**. MTR moved on to 1.21; ATR keeps 1.20.1 going.

**English only for now.** The mod's text and its announcer voices are in English. In another game language, announcements use your computer's own voice.

### Drop-in for MTR
Take out the MTR jar and put ATR in its place. The mod id is still `mtr`, so your MTR 4.0.5 worlds and train packs keep working. Do not run both. MAGIC 1.5.1, MTR Rail Works 0.2.3 and MTR France Addon 2.0.1 were checked against ATR's code, but not yet launched with this build. Tell us if an add-on does not work.

### What you get
- **Station generator**: plan a station or an airport in Station Studio in your browser, then build it in the world with the ATR wrench. Bridges along a rail and earthworks are also wrench modes. City styles build real stations' fronts and halls, for example St Pancras, Utrecht Centraal and West Kowloon. A station on a hillside levels its ground, and undo still works after a restart.
- **Roads**: road tools and a road hub (**B**) with city streets, bus lanes, rural roads and highways, junctions, crossings, and left- or right-hand traffic. Every lane carries hidden MTR track.
- **Passengers**: people wait on the platforms, ride to any station the network reaches and change trains on the way.
- **Trains**: express trains go first at junctions, clock-face timetables, AWS and TPWS, MTR 4.1's train picker, per-train rail sounds and dyeable PIDS.
- **Announcer**: three downloadable English voices, Isabella, Lewis and Michael. Nothing extra goes in the jar.
- **Aurelia panel** (**M**): every switch in one screen, in seven groups.
- **Rail Builder**: one smart tool for quicker builds, and sit or stand while riding.
- **Station blocks**: Aurelia Transit Architecture's station, airport and wayfinding blocks are inside the jar.
- **Map** (**N**): lines, stations and how busy they are, homes and landmarks.
- **Faster**: merged train parts, a shader-pack fast path, cheaper shadows, level of detail for trains and rails, background model loading. Every fix has a switch.
- **Resource Pack Creator**: make train packs in a page that opens from the dashboard. It imports Blender and Blockbench models and works offline.

### Notes
- Station designs copy real stations so you can build them. Operator signs use generic marks, not real logos. Nothing here is endorsed by any operator.
- Fabric 1.20.1 only. Needs Fabric Loader 0.20.1 or newer and Fabric API.
- Based on Minecraft Transit Railway by jonafanho, MIT licensed.
