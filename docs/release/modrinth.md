# Aurelia Transit Railway (Modrinth and CurseForge page text)

**Summary (one line):** A maintained, faster Minecraft Transit Railway for 1.20.1 with building tools, station blocks and a 3D web map. Drop-in for MTR 4.0.5.

## Body
Aurelia Transit Railway (ATR) is a maintained fork of [Minecraft Transit Railway](https://modrinth.com/mod/minecraft-transit-railway) 4.0.5 for **Minecraft 1.20.1 on Fabric**. MTR moved on to 1.21; ATR keeps 1.20.1 going.

### Drop-in for MTR
Take out the MTR jar and put ATR in its place. The mod id is still `mtr`, so your worlds, train packs and add-ons (Joban Client Mod, MAGIC, Tianjin, London Underground) keep working. Do not run both.

### What you get
- **Faster**: merged train parts, shader-pack fast path, cheaper shadows, level of detail for trains and rails, background model loading. Every fix has a switch.
- **Aurelia panel**: all tools and switches in one screen, with the Announcer and platform calls.
- **Rail Builder**: one smart tool, quicker builds, and sit or stand while riding.
- **Station blocks and Station Studio**: Aurelia Transit Architecture's blocks and wrenches are inside the jar.
- **AureliaMap**: a 3D web map with lines, stations and live trains.
- **Fixes**: no rain inside train cars, platform gate maps, airplane landings, lift menu rows.

### Notes
- Includes real-world station and operator looks (for example NS, DB, the Underground roundel). They are there for building and are not endorsed by any operator.
- Fabric 1.20.1 only. Needs Fabric API.
- Based on Minecraft Transit Railway by jonafanho, MIT licensed.
